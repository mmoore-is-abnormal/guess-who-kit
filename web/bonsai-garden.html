      How it works

      • The team grows one tree at a time. Anyone can start a vote (1 hour, 4 hours, 24 hours or 7 days). Everyone picks a tree and a mode, and the top tree and top mode win.
      • When a vote closes, the winner is planted from seed and the old tree retires to the Hall of Fame. The species can't be changed after that, only replaced by the next vote.
      • Care: water and sun every 4 hours per person, prune every 12, feed once a day. Tending a need that's running low earns the most growth.
      • Modes: Zen Garden never dies. Greenhouse drains faster and gets pests below 30% health, and three different teammates have to treat them. Arena adds a weekday streak bonus and titles.
      • Growth milestones unlock cosmetic treasures. Any contributor can decorate; one accessory fits each spot. Each tree keeps its own arrangement, including in the Hall of Fame.
      • Weekends: whoever starts a vote picks whether the next tree's needs pause on weekends or keep draining every day.
      • Easy (Zen Garden): the tree tells you what it needs and greys out anything you've just done. Hard (Greenhouse, Arena): it only hints at its mood and nothing is greyed out. Acting too soon earns no points, and watering wet soil twice in 12 hours waterlogs it. Two teammates can drain the pot, or it dries out in 24 hours (12 with sunlight).
      • Talk to the tree any time, in any mode. It earns 1 growth point once an hour.
